# Implementation plan

Learn to find intermediate goals in latent space that improve final task success. At deployment, the planner receives a public observation and action history, a terminal objective and the remaining action budget. A world model proposes candidate goals by completing the latent trajectory between the history and the terminal condition. A selector estimates the value of attempting each candidate: the probability of terminal success after the shared executor pursues it and the shared continuation uses the actual remaining budget. [Research plan](docs/latent-subgoal-research.md)

The paper's central claim is that a goal is worth reaching because of what attempting it does to terminal success. Model likelihood, arrival probability, route length, latent distance to a goal example and trajectory-level success labels measure different things. The experiments test this claim with predeclared candidates and matched interventions, then show that latent proposals plus a budget-conditioned value can compose reliable routes that demonstrations rarely contain. [Positioning against prior work](docs/latent-subgoal-research.md#positioning-against-prior-work)

## Completed evidence

| Study | Result | Record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dependency grid benchmark | On 16 development layouts, swapping the goal reversed the ranking of the two lane-specific ordered targets on every layout. The goal-matched order succeeded on 511 of 512 attempts, the reversed order on 199 and local direct tracking on 112. | [Benchmark](docs/latent-subgoal-research.md#1-establish-a-controlled-multi-stage-benchmark) |
| Frozen energy versus uniform choice | Minimum flow energy and uniform selection were within noise on development layouts for both model seeds. Reference-selected flow candidates reached 46.5% and 62.5%, so the pool contained useful targets that the frozen score missed. | [Development comparison](docs/latent-subgoal-research.md#2-separate-discovery-selection-and-execution) |
| Outcome selection on test layouts | Outcome scoring raised two-stage latent success over minimum energy by 18.75 and 25.00 points for the two model seeds. The intervals were [6.25, 34.38] and [9.38, 43.75]. | [Confirmation](docs/latent-subgoal-research.md#2-separate-discovery-selection-and-execution) |
| Budgeted route composition on test worlds | At four actions, composition reached 93.16% against 84.38% for joint samples, single targets and replay. The gain was 8.79 points [1.17, 16.80]. Composition confirmed 12 low-support paths per seed, and the selected route changed with the budget on 8 of 8 worlds. | [Confirmation](docs/latent-subgoal-research.md#3-compose-alternative-routes-under-a-budget) |
| Scene executor diagnostic (development, provisional) | Across six executor checkpoints at different scales, drawer insertion within 128 actions rose from 9 of 108 to 39 of 108 validation clips. The executor is not yet qualified for the terminal comparison. | [Waypoint protocol](docs/experiments.md#test-scene-drawer-insertion-in-isolation) |

## Experiment infrastructure

The paper uses these public entry points.

| Command | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `wr dependency-grid`, `wr dependency-grid-latent` | Benchmark checks, latent proposals, frozen energy, and outcome selection with `--selector-repeats`. [Grid protocol](docs/experiments.md#compare-ordered-targets-in-the-dependency-grid) |
| `wr budget-routes` | Budget-conditioned composition, fixed-pool route changes, rare-path confirmation and suffix replanning. [Routes protocol](docs/experiments.md#compose-latent-goals-under-action-budgets) |
| `wr attempt-compare` | Selector comparison on `shared_attempts_v1` banks. The base for E2. [Selector comparison](docs/experiments.md#compare-selectors-on-matched-attempts) |
| `wr scene-latent`, `wr scene-evaluate`, `wr scene-reachability` | Scene training, terminal evaluation and executor qualification. [Scene protocol](docs/experiments.md#run-state-based-ogbench-scene) |
| `wr pointmaze-latent`, `wr pointmaze-first-targets`, `wr pointmaze-latent-energy` | Maze2D planning, budget sweeps and frozen-energy tests. [Maze2D protocol](docs/experiments.md#run-the-diffuser-maze2d-pilots) |

The shared masked trajectory completion model supplies the proposer for every task. It trains a public-history encoder and predictor to complete the hidden middle representations from both ends, then samples alternatives with residual flow matching. [Trajectory completion design](docs/trajectory-completion.md) · [Training protocol](docs/experiments.md#train-and-test-latent-trajectory-completion)

These runners remain available but are outside the paper's main line: Craftax window export and collection, KeyCorridor replay, the two-goal grid and its feedback training (`wr grid-feedback`), AntMaze GCBC and latent planning, Kuka stacking and Puzzle. The feedback runner is the starting point for the P2 generator update. [Running experiments](docs/experiments.md) · [Environments](docs/experiment_env.md)

## Evaluation protocol

Keep exploratory development worlds separate from test worlds. Freeze decision rules before the final comparison. Declare candidates and scores in a candidate bank before rollouts. Compare methods on matched starts and independent repeat streams. When isolating proposal or selection quality, share the executor, continuation, terminal objective and candidate budget. Record all environment interactions used for training, selector fitting, proposal updates and evaluation, and give baselines the same interaction allowance when they can use it.
